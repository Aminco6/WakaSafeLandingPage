Account Deletion Request

This page explains how to request deletion of your WakaSafe account and associated data. You can also use the form below to submit a request directly.

📋 Steps to Request Account Deletion

To delete your WakaSafe account and associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Open the WakaSafe app on your Android device and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to Settings — tap the gear icon ⚙️ or go to the “Settings” section from the dashboard.
  3. Select “Account” or “Delete Account” — look for the option labelled “Delete Account” or “Request Account Deletion”.
  4. Confirm your request — you will be asked to enter your PIN or password to verify your identity.
  5. Submit the request — once confirmed, your account will be scheduled for deletion. You will receive a confirmation email or SMS.
Can’t access the app? No problem. Use the form below to submit a deletion request manually. We will process it within 7 working days.

🗂️ Data That Is Deleted or Kept

When you request account deletion, the following applies:

Data Type Action Retention Period
Account profile (name, phone, email) Deleted Within 7 working days
Emergency contacts list Deleted Within 7 working days
Journey history & location logs Deleted Within 7 working days
SMS broadcast logs Deleted Within 7 working days
PIN & security settings Deleted Within 7 working days
Referral code & usage data Deleted Within 7 working days
Anonymised analytics (no personal identifiers) Kept Up to 12 months (for service improvement)
Transaction records (if subscribed) Kept As required by Nigerian financial regulations (up to 7 years)
Note: Some data may be retained for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes as required by Nigerian law. This data is kept securely and not used for any other purpose.
